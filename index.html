MURIWAI QUEST

A field-research RPG for Geography 3.1 (91426)

You've been sent to survey the Muriwai coastal environment. Walk the coast, question the locals, and gather the field data an Excellence essay needs — then defend it in front of the Examiner.

←↑→↓ or WASD — walk
Space / Enter — talk to whoever you're facing
On touch devices, use the on-screen pad below the map.

You have an expedition in progress.

HOW TO PLAY

←↑→↓ / WASD — walk one tile
Space / Enter — talk to the character you're facing
Rock falls and driftwood block a path until you've solved every question in that area.
Correct answers add a Field Data card to your journal (right-hand panel) — you'll need them later.
Wrong answers just explain the gap and let you try again — no penalty.

RESET PROGRESS?

This clears all field data, XP, and your position, and starts the expedition over from Muriwai Beach. This can't be undone.

PREVIEW & JUMP TO ZONE

Preview mode leaves every barrier open and shows every field-data card at the Examiner, so you can walk straight to any part of the expedition. NPC questions still show their real content — nothing is pre-answered — so you can check them properly. Jump to a zone below, or just walk once preview is on.

Jumping to a zone turns preview mode on automatically, so the walk there is never blocked.
